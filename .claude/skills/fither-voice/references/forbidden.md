# Never appears in FITHER content

Merged from the app repo's forbidden list (fither-domain, applied to words)
and the channel rules. This is a hard list, not a guideline. It applies to
scripts, hooks, captions, hashtags, comment replies, and any store copy.

## Body and weight

- weight, weigh-in, weight loss, pounds, kilos, scale, fat, slim, slimming, skinny
- tone, tone up, sculpt, lean out, shred, torch, melt, snatch(ed)
- bikini, bikini body, summer body, snapback, problem areas
- before and after, transformation photos, any before/after framing
- comparison to other women

## Food and calories

- calories, burn, burn off, earn your food, work it off, guilt-free
- diet, cheat meal, cheat day, guilt

## Shame and streaks

- streak, chain, "don't break", "you missed a day", "we miss you",
  "don't lose your progress"
- "no excuses", "what's stopping you", any shame-adjacent motivation
- "just a quick one", "mini workout" (ten minutes is complete)

## Claims

- any claim to treat, cure, fix or heal an injury or condition
  ("wrist-friendly" describes a movement; "fixes wrist pain" is a claim)
- "transform your body in N days" or any transformation promise

## Bro-speak and persona breaks

- "crush it", "beast mode", "queen", "girlie", "slay", "you got this"
- unexplained acronyms: AMRAP, HIIT, EMOM

## Channel integrity

- exercises not in `movement-library.md`
- the app, the waitlist, the launch — during the entire warm-up period
- a human face in stock or owned footage, in any scene (generated footage is
  the one exception, owner override 2026-09-03: the person is synthetic)
- casting that reads as a fitness model. The recurring character is an
  ordinary woman, because "comparison to other women" above is about who she
  is, not what she wears.
