# Cocktail cover hooks

Candidate hooks for the cocktail carousel's slide one: a real person, face
covered, doing something ordinary, with the hook set loud over it. Unproven.
Nothing here has been posted, so treat the whole file as a hypothesis bank,
not a proven set. Winners graduate to
`.claude/skills/fither-voice/references/hooks.md` with their numbers after
they earn it.

Every line passes `references/forbidden.md`. That is the constraint that
makes this hard: the reference account farms with "toned belly", "no
excuses" and "dream body", and none of those are available. What is left is
recognition, constraint and arithmetic, which pull a different woman.

**Pairing matters more than the line.** The hook lands because the picture
under it is not a gym. Each hook below names the shot it sits on.

---

## 1. The gap she already has

The strongest lane for this audience: she is not short of willpower, she is
short of time. The picture is the gap, the hook names it.

| Hook | Shot underneath |
|---|---|
| The workout that fits in the time the kettle takes | Hand pressing the kettle switch, steam starting |
| 10 minutes while dinner cooks. Six moves. | Oven door half open, tea towel over the shoulder |
| Your lunch break is a full session | Laptop lid closing, sandwich still wrapped |
| The 8 minutes between bath time and bedtime | Bathroom doorway, towel over the arm, toys on the floor |
| Train in the time it takes to reheat leftovers | Microwave door, back to camera |
| The session that fits inside one podcast episode | Earbuds going in, hallway mirror |

## 2. Recognition, then a turn

Name the moment she recognises, then contradict the conclusion she drew from
it. The silly picture does the recognition so the hook can do the turn.

| Hook | Shot underneath |
|---|---|
| You cancelled again because your wrists hurt. Six moves never touch them. | Wrist being rubbed, kettle in the other hand |
| Sore wrists are not a rest day | Hands flat on a kitchen counter, resting rather than pressing |
| You are not bad at push-ups. You started on the wrong step. | Palms against a wall, sideways |
| Nobody fails push-ups. They skip steps. | Same wall, feet in socks |
| You do not need a gym. You need a doorframe. | Both hands gripping a doorframe, leaning back |
| Everyone trains their front. Nothing trains her back. | Back to camera, shoulder blades together |
| You keep waiting for a free hour. It is not coming. | Phone showing a full calendar, coffee going cold |

## 4. The silly opener with a hard turn

The lane closest to the reference. The picture is deliberately not fitness;
the hook makes the turn on slide two. Keep the joke dry, not zany: the voice
is a competent adult, not a skit account.

| Hook | Shot underneath |
|---|---|
| This is my gym. It has a kettle. | Standing in a kitchen, arms folded, face out of frame |
| Equipment list: one wall | Hand patting a bare wall like a car bonnet |
| My personal trainer is the oven timer | Finger setting a timer |
| The doorframe is doing more for my back than any gym did | Hanging off a doorframe, feet planted |
| I do this in the 9 minutes my coffee takes to go cold | Full mug on a table, sitting down beside it |
| Nobody in this house knows I trained | Hallway at night, door closed, socks |
| Cardigan on. Still counts. | Sitting on a chair edge in normal clothes |

## 5. The reframe

Highest brand fit and the lane the account should own long term. Lowest
immediate pull, because it asks for a thought rather than promising a result.

| Hook | Shot underneath |
|---|---|
| Train to carry, not to shrink | Two shopping bags on a doorstep |
| Strong is a capacity, not a look | Hand on a stair rail, bag on the shoulder |
| Your shoulders carry your actual week | Rucksack going on, back to camera |
| Nobody carries a toddler by being smaller | Car seat handle in one hand |
| Stairs are the test. Train for the test. | Feet mid staircase, bags in hand |

## How to use this

- **One hook per post, on slide one, nothing else on that slide.** The
  reference wins because the cover is a single readable claim.
- **Do not put the number in the picture and the hook.** Redundancy costs the
  hook its room.
- **Rotate lanes.** Seven cocktails a week should not be seven number posts.
  Section 3 farms search, section 4 farms the feed, section 5 builds the
  brand. A week that is all section 3 is a week with no personality.
- **The turn happens on slide two.** The cover is lifestyle. Slide two is the
  first movement, named, with the rep count.

## What is deliberately absent

No "before", no "results", no "days", no body part described as a goal.
"Glute workout" names a muscle a movement trains and is fine; "toned belly"
describes a body being judged and is not. The line between them is whether
the words describe the work or the woman.
