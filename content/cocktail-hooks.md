# Cocktail cover hooks

The cover of a cocktail carousel is **not about fitness.** It is a real woman,
face hidden, doing something ordinary, with one sassy line about her actual
life set loud over it. The turn to the movements happens on slide two. This
file is the cover line, the shot underneath it, and the turn line.

Unproven. Nothing here has posted. Winners graduate to
`.claude/skills/fither-voice/references/hooks.md` with their numbers.

How this set was made: ten writers on ten comedic angles drafted 120 lines;
two skeptics refuted anything with fitness on the cover or anything off-voice
(35 fell); three judges scored the rest on thumb-stop, voice and whether the
turn is earned. This is everything scoring 6 or above out of 10, and every
line then passed the policy checker. Scores are in brackets; a 7 was rare by
design.

**She is:** 28 to 45, more responsibility than time, dry, self-aware, not a
beginner and not in need of cheering up. Sassy means the funniest woman in the
office at 4pm on a Thursday. Not perky, not girlboss, not a skit account.

## Big plan, tiny reality

A grand claim, then the sock.

**Told the group chat I am thriving. From the floor.** (7.3)  
Shot: Bedroom. Lying flat on the carpet beside the bed, phone held straight up above her face blocking it, feet propped on the mattress edge, work shoes still on.  
Turn: Already on the floor. Glute bridges. Ten minutes, then reply.

**Business trip. Glamorous. Steaming a blouse in the hotel shower.** (6.7)  
Shot: Hotel room. Back to camera at the bathroom door, hanging a creased blouse on the shower rail, conference lanyard on, suitcase open on the bed behind her.  
Turn: The steam takes ten minutes. So does the wall.

**Left the house on time. Came back for keys, phone, child.** (6.3)  
Shot: Hallway. Back to camera, coat on, one hand on the front door latch, keys still hanging on the hook behind her, a small shoe on the floor.  
Turn: Six moves in the hallway. Nothing to forget this time.

**Bins out. Neighbour waved. Still in the dressing gown.** (6.3)  
Shot: Doorstep. Back to camera on the front step in a dressing gown and trainers, one hand on the wheelie bin handle, other hand raised in a small wave at nobody.  
Turn: The front door holds a push up. Ten, then coffee.

**Grown woman, own house. Socks from the dryer, still damp.** (6.0)  
Shot: Kitchen. Crouched at the open tumble dryer pulling out a single sock, face hidden behind the dryer door, a basket of unpaired socks on the floor beside her.  
Turn: The dryer runs twenty minutes. You need ten. Squats.


## Petty

Small grudges, precisely held. The printer, the neighbour, the dishwasher.

**Sat in the car so nobody asks me for anything.** (7.0)  
Shot: Car. Driver seat, engine off, sun visor down hiding her face, both hands resting on the wheel, a child seat visible behind.  
Turn: Seated knee lifts. Twenty. Nobody can see you here.

**The dishwasher is full and nobody else can see it.** (6.7)  
Shot: Kitchen. Standing at the open dishwasher, face hidden behind the raised cupboard door above it, one mug in hand, bottom rack pulled out.  
Turn: The bottom rack is a hinge. Do ten more first.

**Someone is knocking on the bathroom door. Someone always is.** (6.3)  
Shot: Bathroom doorway. Standing in the frame with a towel wrapped over her head and face, one hand flat on the doorframe, toothbrush in the other.  
Turn: Doorframe rows. Ten. Then open it.

**My neighbour waves like I owe him money.** (6.3)  
Shot: Doorstep. Back to camera, keys in hand, one bin wheeled beside her, the open front door edge covering the side of her face.  
Turn: Wave back. Air squats in the hallway. He cannot see those.

**The washing machine beeps at me like I work for it.** (6.0)  
Shot: Hallway utility corner. Back to camera, laundry basket on one hip, staring down at the machine display, one sock on the floor.  
Turn: It beeps in six minutes. Standing hinges until then.


## Office

Open-plan absurdity: cameras off, synergy, the printer walk.

**Walked to the printer to have a feeling.** (7.0)  
Shot: Hallway. Standing still with a stack of printouts held up against her chest and chin, face behind the top sheet, one shoe half off.  
Turn: Ten Reverse Lunges on the way back. Nobody looks up.

**Camera off means I am still in bed.** (6.3)  
Shot: Bedroom. Sitting up in bed, duvet to the waist, blazer over pyjamas, laptop on her knees, phone held up covering her face.  
Turn: Sit-to-Stand. The edge of the bed counts as a chair.

**Took the call in the bathroom. It was about synergy.** (6.3)  
Shot: Bathroom doorway. Propped against the doorframe, phone to her ear, hand towel over one shoulder, hair fallen across her face.  
Turn: That doorframe does rows. Ten minutes, no synergy.

**Replied all. Regretted all.** (6.0)  
Shot: Kitchen. Standing at the counter, phone face up in one hand, forehead resting against a closed cupboard door, kettle light on.  
Turn: Ten Air Squats before you open the replies.


## Travel

Hotel kettles, half-packed suitcases, room 412.

**Ironed a shirt with the hotel kettle. Twice this year.** (7.0)  
Shot: Hotel room, back to camera, holding a steaming kettle up to a shirt on a hanger hooked over the wardrobe door.  
Turn: Three minutes to boil. Wall push ups until it clicks.

**Home for eleven hours. Nine of them asleep.** (6.7)  
Shot: Doorstep at night, suitcase beside her, head down hunting for the key in a coat pocket, hair over her face, porch light on.  
Turn: Ten of those minutes. The hallway wall. Then bed.

**The suitcase has lived on the bedroom floor since June.** (6.3)  
Shot: Sitting cross legged on the bedroom floor next to the half packed suitcase, the open lid hiding her face, one hand dropping in a charger.  
Turn: Step over it. Ten air squats. Call it unpacking.

**Expense report rejected. The receipt was for a sandwich.** (6.0)  
Shot: Office desk, holding a crumpled receipt up in front of her face with one hand, the other still on the keyboard, lanyard on.  
Turn: Stand up from that chair twenty times. That is a set.


## The 9pm text

**Hid in the bathroom for four minutes. Nobody noticed.** (7.0)  
Shot: Bathroom doorway. Sitting on the closed loo lid, door a few inches open, hair over her face, phone in both hands.  
Turn: Four minutes fits a wall sit and twenty squats.

**Got into bed at 8.40 and I regret nothing.** (6.3)  
Shot: Bedroom. Duvet pulled up to the nose, phone held above her face, lamp on, laptop still open on the other pillow.

**Sat in the car outside my own house for twenty minutes.** (6.3)  
Shot: Car. Driver's seat, engine off, head tipped back against the headrest, face hidden by the sun visor, house lights on through the windscreen.  
Turn: Ten of them are a session. Start by the car door.

**Room 412 has one chair and my full attention.** (6.3)

## POV

Meme grammar for a 38 year old, not a teenager.

**Me sitting in the driveway because inside has people in it.** (7.0)  
Shot: Car, parked. Head tipped back on the headrest, sun visor down hiding the face, engine off, a bag for life on the passenger seat.  
Turn: Five minutes here. Ten at the wall inside. Then people.

**POV: the toothbrush is the longest you have stood still today.** (6.0)  
Shot: Bathroom doorway. Back to camera, hair up in a clip, toothbrush in mouth, one hand on the doorframe, towel over the shoulder.  
Turn: Two minutes brushing. Two minutes of Wall Sit. Same clock.

**Me sitting on the stairs because it was nearest.** (6.0)  
Shot: Stairs. Sitting three steps up, elbows on knees, hair falling forward over the face, a laundry basket parked on the step beside her.  
Turn: Stand up, sit down, ten times. That is the first set.

**Me signing for a parcel I do not remember ordering.** (6.0)  
Shot: Doorstep. Back to camera in socks, taking a cardboard box from the open front door, one foot still inside the house.  
Turn: Put it down. That doorframe does rows. Ten minutes.

**Me eating the crusts standing up. This is dinner now.** (6.0)  
Shot: Kitchen. Standing at the counter over a small plastic plate, back to camera, one hand holding a crust, the other scrolling.  
Turn: Dinner took ninety seconds. The hinge at that counter takes ten.


## Confession

Unhinged honesty from a competent adult who is out of hours.

**My coffee is on its third reheat and so am I.** (6.7)  
Shot: Standing at the microwave, face hidden by the open door, mug in hand, dressing gown over office shirt.  
Turn: Ninety seconds on the microwave. Ninety seconds of air squats.

**I have sat in the car for twenty minutes because it is quiet.** (6.0)  
Shot: Parked in the driveway, hands on the wheel, head tipped back, hair covering the face, engine off.  
Turn: Twenty quiet minutes. Ten of them can be sit-to-stands on the doorstep.

**Hotel room. Nobody needs me. I do not know what to do.** (6.0)  
Shot: Standing in a hotel room with the blackout curtain half open, back to the camera, still in the coat, suitcase unopened.  
Turn: One wall, one chair, ten minutes. That is what you do.

**Went to bed at nine. Lay there and thought about emails.** (6.0)  
Shot: Sitting up in bed in the dark, duvet to the chin, phone glow on the ceiling, face turned into the pillow.  
Turn: Six moves in the morning. The emails can watch.


## Small children

The lock is decorative. Never shame, always dry.

**I have been asked for a snack four times since I sat down.** (6.0)  
Shot: At the kitchen table with a cold coffee, back to the camera, a small hand reaching up from below the table edge.  
Turn: Snack five is a hinge. Ten Standing Hip Hinges, then the crackers.


## Anti-wellness

Mocking the routine, never the woman. 5am clubs, digital detoxes.

**Digital detox starts after this one email.** (6.7)  
Shot: Office desk. Laptop open, phone in the other hand, head tipped back against the chair so only the chin and the ceiling light show.  
Turn: Send it. Then ten minutes, laptop closed, one wall.

**I journaled today. It was the meeting notes.** (6.0)  
Shot: Office desk. Head down writing in a notebook, mug held up in front of the face, laptop open with a calendar full of colour blocks.  
Turn: Page two is ten minutes. Twelve times up from that chair.

## How to use this

- **One line on slide one, nothing else.** The cover is a single readable claim.
- **The shot is the joke's evidence.** Film the shot as written; the line only
  lands because the picture is her life and not a gym.
- **The turn goes on slide two**, over the first movement card. It reuses the
  cover's own image (the kettle, the car, the printer) so the pivot is earned.
- **Rotate lanes.** Seven covers a week from one lane is a bit, not a channel.
- **Face hidden in every shot**: phone, hair, visor, door edge, towel, crop.

## What is deliberately absent

No fitness on the cover, at all. No body talk, no shape, no comparison, no
shame, no cheerleading, no product. The reference account farms with all of
those; this set farms with recognition instead. See `references/forbidden.md`
for the literal list.
