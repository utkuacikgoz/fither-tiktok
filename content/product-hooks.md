# Product hooks

Twenty attention lines built on the product promise itself: strength that
fits her actual day. Warm-up rule applies in full, so none of them name the
app, a waitlist or a launch. They sell the idea, and the account earns the
right to sell the thing later.

Every line passed `pipeline/lib/policy.mjs` on 2026-09-16. Unproven until
posted; winners graduate to `references/hooks.md` with their numbers.

## The gym you already have

1. Your gym is the wall by the window. It always was.
2. A door frame is a pull machine. Ten rows, then coffee.
3. Hotel room, no floor space, one wall. That is enough.
4. Half of it happens in a hotel. Your strength should travel.

## Ten minutes is complete

5. Ten minutes is a complete session. Nobody told you that.
6. You do not need a spare hour. You have never had one.
7. Between two meetings there are twelve minutes. That is a session.
8. Your calendar just gave you a workout.
9. She naps for forty minutes. Ten of them are yours.

## Invisible training

10. Nobody in that meeting knows you trained at 2:50.
11. Someone is asleep on the other side of that wall. Train anyway, in silence.
12. The floor creaks. She trains anyway.
13. You train in work clothes at 3pm. Still a real session.

## Life is the test

14. The stairs with the buggy already trained you. Now train for them.
15. You lifted the case into the boot. That was a hinge. You are already training.
16. Strong enough to carry her home is a real goal. Chase that one.
17. The lift is broken again. Good. Two flights with the shopping is a session.

## It adapts, you do not skip

18. Sore wrists are not a rest day. Pull, squat, hinge, core.
19. Tired is not the end of the session. It is where the plan changes.
20. Same three moves every day of the worst week. That counts.

## Borrowed formats, translated

Proven TikTok fitness hook shapes (owner list, 2026-09-16), rewritten so the
mechanism survives and the forbidden list holds. "Get ripped", "abs",
"nice back" and "fix your posture" are body or claim language and could not
ship as written; the versions below keep the curiosity, drop the promise.
