# Sore wrists, train everything else

- Topic: What to train when your wrists hurt, and the honest admission that no
  push-up variation spares them.
- Pillar: Constraint
- Format: slideshow (seven slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-09-05 (Wednesday)
- Target search phrase: workout when wrists hurt no equipment

## 2. SCRIPT (seven slides)

1. SORE WRISTS. "Sore wrists are not a rest day. They are a different session."
2. WHY. "Every push loads your wrists. Pull, squat, hinge and core do not."
3. PULL. "Doorframe Row, eight reps. Your back does the work. Your wrists stay out."
4. LEGS. "Sit-to-Stand, eight reps. Wall Sit, thirty seconds. Air Squat, ten reps."
5. HIPS AND CORE. "Glute Bridge, ten reps. Standing Hip Hinge, ten reps. Knee Plank, twenty seconds."
6. THE HONEST BIT. "No push-up leaves your wrists out. When they ache, you skip pushing."
7. YOUR TURN. "Which move starts your session?"

Footers carry the two capability lines the voice asks for: "You build this."
on the pull slide, "Your strength travels with you." on the hips and core
slide. The close is "Ten minutes is complete." before the question.

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. Six movements, every one in
`movement-library.md`, and every one loading something other than the wrists.
The library's own Loads column is the check:

| Movement | Loads |
|---|---|
| Doorframe Row | back, shoulders, elbows |
| Sit-to-Stand | knees, hips |
| Wall Sit | knees, hips |
| Air Squat | knees, hips, ankles |
| Glute Bridge | hips |
| Standing Hip Hinge | hips, back |
| Knee Plank | core, shoulders |

The planks are forearm variants, which is why they belong here and a Wall
Push-Up does not. Single-Arm Doorframe Row is excluded deliberately: the
library lists wrists among its loads.

Slide 6 exists because the movement library carries no push variant that
spares the wrists, and the honest move is to say so rather than dress a
kneeling push-up up as a solution. Nothing here claims to treat, fix or heal
anything: sore wrists mean you change what you train, not that a session
repairs a joint.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Sore wrists are not a rest day. Every push loads the wrist. Pull, squat, hinge
and core do not. Six moves, ten minutes, nothing to hold and nothing to push.
Save this for the next sore day.
