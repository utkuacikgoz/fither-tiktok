# Desk body reset (carousel)

- Topic: Desk-worker relief using real strength movements, not stretches.
- Pillar: Constraint
- Format: slideshow (7 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-09-16
- Target search phrase: desk exercises for sitting all day

## Why strength moves, not stretches

The ask was "stretching tips for desk workers." `movement-library.md` has no
stretch-pattern entries, since it is a strength library rather than a
mobility one, so naming specific stretches (neck rolls, wrist circles) would
violate movement truth: nothing backs them. The same desk-relief need is served instead by
five real library movements that counter sitting: two that open the chest
and upper back, one hip hinge, and two that just ask the legs and core to
work after being still.

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Your body is not built to sit for eight hours straight." (situation: names
the shared condition before offering the fix.)

## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. SIT ALL DAY. "Your body is not built to sit for eight hours straight."
2. SHOULDERS. "Shoulder Blade Squeeze. Twelve reps, sitting tall."
3. UPPER BACK. "Wall Slide. Ten reps against the nearest wall."
4. HIPS. "Standing Hip Hinge. Ten reps, stand up first."
5. LEGS. "Sit-to-Stand. Eight reps, no hands on the desk."
6. CORE. "Seated Knee Lift. Ten each side, right where you are."
7. YOUR TURN. "Which one are you doing right now?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only. Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library
dose: Shoulder Blade Squeeze (12 reps, back/shoulders), Wall Slide (10 reps,
back/shoulders), Standing Hip Hinge (10 reps, hips/back), Sit-to-Stand
(8 reps, knees/hips), Seated Knee Lift (10 each side, core/hips).

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Your body is not built to sit for eight hours straight. Five moves at your
desk: Shoulder Blade Squeeze, Wall Slide, Standing Hip Hinge, Sit-to-Stand,
Seated Knee Lift. No floor, no changing, no equipment. Save this for your
next meeting.
