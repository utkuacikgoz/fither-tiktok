## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. 11PM. "If it is 11pm and the day gave you nothing, start here."
2. ONE. "Glute Bridge. 10 reps. Lie on the floor, feet flat, push your hips up."
3. TWO. "Lying Heel Slide. 10 reps. Stay on your back and slide one heel out and back."
4. THREE. "Sit-to-Stand. 8 reps. Sit on the bed edge and stand up without your hands."
5. FOUR. "Seated Knee Lift. 10 reps. Back on the bed edge, sit tall, lift one knee at a time."
6. FIVE. "Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Hands on the wall by the bed, lower your chest in, press back."
7. THE STRUCTURE. "Two rounds of the five. Ten minutes, and you never leave the room."
8. YOUR TURN. "What is your 11pm move?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. Three slides carry the authored figure, which is
the movement demo for a photo post: Glute Bridge, Seated Knee Lift and Wall
Push-Up all have approved exports in `assets/animation-library.json`. Lying
Heel Slide and Sit-to-Stand are text only, so the instruction sentence does
the work the figure would.

The scene never leaves one bedroom. The floor takes two movements, the bed
edge acts as the chair for two more, and the wall beside the bed takes the
last one, which is why the list needs nothing bought and nothing moved.
Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library dose: Glute Bridge
(hinge, tier 1, 10 reps), Lying Heel Slide (core, tier 1, 10 reps),
Sit-to-Stand (squat, tier 2, chair, 8 reps), Seated Knee Lift (core, tier 1,
chair, 10 reps), Wall Push-Up (push, tier 1, wall, 10 reps).

Slide seven is the one that makes it saveable: it turns five named movements
into a session with an end, at the hour when deciding is the hard part.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Quiet workout beside the bed at 11pm: Glute Bridge, Lying Heel Slide, Sit-to-Stand off the bed edge, Seated Knee Lift, Wall Push-Up. Floor and bed edge only, no jumping, nothing that wakes the house. Save this for the night the day gave you nothing else.
