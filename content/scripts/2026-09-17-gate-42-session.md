# Gate 42, ninety minutes

- Topic: A 10 minute standing and gate-seat strength session in the clothes she is flying in, done at the departure gate without changing or using the floor.
- Pillar: Constraint
- Format: motion typography (45 to 60s). Brand-ground cards only. No footage, no demo, no animation.
- Posting date: 2026-09-17 (Thursday)
- Target search phrase: layover workout without changing clothes

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

First frame: the hook card on the brand ground, already moving. There is no
footage in this piece by decision, so the first frame is the strongest line
set in the largest type. The card is the caption: the words on screen are the
words being spoken.

1. "Gate 42. Ninety minutes. A row of strangers opposite." (specific
   situation: the named gate, the named span of time, the named audience.
   CHOSEN, and registered as one of EXP-007's named-constraint hooks)
2. "You are not short of time here. You are short of privacy." (contradiction:
   the constraint everyone assumes against the one that actually stops her)
3. "Five moves at the gate, and nobody opposite looks up." (number/promise)

## 2. SCRIPT (about 60 seconds)

0:00 Hook: "Gate 42. Ninety minutes. A row of strangers opposite."

0:05 "You are not short of time. You are short of privacy."

0:12 "No floor. No changing. Nothing anyone notices."

0:16 "Sit-to-Stand from the gate seat. Eight reps."

0:21 "Seated Knee Lift. Ten reps. Nobody looks up."

0:25 "Stand up. Standing Hip Hinge. Ten slow reps."

0:30 "Wall Slide on the window pillar. Ten reps."

0:35 "Shoulder Blade Squeeze. Twelve reps. Almost nothing moves."

0:40 Payoff: "Ten minutes. A complete session. Nobody looked up."

0:45 Capability: "You trained in what you are flying in."

0:49 Capability: "Your strength boards with you."

0:53 Seed tomorrow: "Tomorrow: lifting your own bag overhead."

0:56 CTA: "How long is your layover?"

The loop: the row of strangers opens it, the invisible session answers it,
and the CTA asks her how long her own layover is. The tension is social, not
physical, so every beat escalates how little anyone can tell rather than how
hard it gets. Two capability-ownership lines land before tomorrow's overhead
locker is seeded.
