## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. IF YOU DO THIS. "Ten hip hinges a day and the laundry basket stops being a thing."
2. RUNG ONE. "Glute Bridge. 10 reps. Lie on your back, feet flat, push your hips up."
3. RUNG TWO. "Standing Hip Hinge. 10 reps. Push your hips back, keep your back flat, stand up."
4. RUNG THREE. "Hinge and Reach. 10 reps. Reach both hands down your shins, then stand tall."
5. RUNG FOUR. "Single-Leg Hip Hinge. 6 each side. Hinge on one leg, back leg straight behind you."
6. THE TOP. "Single-Leg Hip Thrust. 6 each side. Shoulders on the chair, one foot down, drive up."
7. HONEST NOTE. "The top rung takes months, not a week."
8. YOUR TURN. "Which rung are you on?"

Every footer on slides 2 to 6 carries the exact test that says she is ready
for the next rung, which is the part the voiceover has to compress.
