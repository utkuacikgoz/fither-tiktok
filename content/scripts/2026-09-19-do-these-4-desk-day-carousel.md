# Do these 4, desk day (carousel)

- Topic: The four move day for a desk that never lets you up, as a saveable list.
- Pillar: Constraint
- Format: slideshow (8 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-09-19
- Target search phrase: desk day workout with no equipment

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Do these 4 every day this week if the desk is your whole day." (situation
mechanism, the named-constraint cover the series runs on: the constraint is
the desk, the number is the promise, and slide one decides nothing else for
her.)

## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. DO THESE 4. "Do these 4 every day this week if the desk is your whole day."
2. ONE. "Sit-to-Stand. 8 reps. Out of the desk chair with no hands, sit back slowly."
3. TWO. "Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Hands on the wall, elbows back, chest in and out."
4. THREE. "Seated Knee Lift. 10 each side. Sit tall, lift one knee, lower it slowly."
5. FOUR. "Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Hold the frame, lean back, pull your chest to your hands."
6. WHAT THIS SKIPS. "No hinge this week. It comes back on a day with a floor to lie down on."
7. THE STRUCTURE. "Two rounds of the four. About eight minutes, no changing clothes."
8. YOUR TURN. "Which one would you do first?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage, so the deck renders locally and ships the same
day. Slides three, four and five carry the authored figure at its peak pose
(`figure: true`), which is what turns a list into a demo she can copy from
the chair: Wall Push-Up, Seated Knee Lift and Doorframe Row all have approved
exports in `assets/animation-library.json`. Slide two stays typographic
because Sit-to-Stand has none yet, and an unverified stand-in is never worth
a slide.

Slide six is the honest slide. Four movements out of a desk chair cover
squat, push, core and pull, and they do not cover hinge, so the deck says so
and says when hinge comes back rather than quietly dropping a pattern. Every
movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library dose: Sit-to-Stand
(squat, tier 2, chair, 8 reps), Wall Push-Up (push, tier 1, wall, 10 reps),
Seated Knee Lift (core, tier 1, chair, 10 reps), Doorframe Row (pull, tier 3,
wall, 8 reps).

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Desk day workout with no equipment: four movements, eight minutes, no changing clothes. Sit-to-Stand, Wall Push-Up, Seated Knee Lift and Doorframe Row, all within reach of the chair you are already in. Save this for the week you never leave the desk.
