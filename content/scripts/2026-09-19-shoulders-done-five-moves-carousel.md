# Shoulders done, five moves (carousel)

- Topic: Five strength moves for a day when her shoulders are finished from carrying, as a saveable list.
- Pillar: Fast tips
- Format: slideshow (8 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-09-19
- Target search phrase: what to train when your shoulders are sore
- Experiment note: EXP-007 number-led control. Slide one opens on the count and
  the structure, not on a named scene. This is one of the three control posts
  the named-constraint group is measured against, so the opening card must stay
  number-led even though a scene line was available.

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Five moves today. None of them touch your shoulders." (the video's hook, held
as slide one so a viewer who scrolls past the video meets the same promise.)

## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. FIVE MOVES. "Five moves today. None of them touch your shoulders."
2. ONE, SQUAT. "Wall Sit, thirty seconds. Knees and hips hold you, shoulders stay out."
3. TWO, SQUAT. "Reverse Lunge, six reps. Slow, quiet, one leg back at a time."
4. THREE, HINGE. "Glute Bridge March, ten reps. Hips lift, core holds the line."
5. FOUR, CORE. "Lying Heel Tap, ten reps. Ribs down, arms doing nothing."
6. FIVE, SQUAT. "Sit-to-Stand, eight reps. Off the chair, no hands, no push."
7. THE HONEST BIT. "No push, no pull today. Both load the shoulder girdle." Footer: "It trains what is fresh."
8. YOUR TURN. "What is tired on you today?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage and no photo. Every movement is named in full with
its library dose, which is what the voiceover had to compress. Slide 7 is the
saveable part: it says plainly which patterns are out today and why, and it
makes no claim about treating, relieving or shortening soreness. It trains
what is fresh, and that is the whole promise.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

What to train when your shoulders are sore: Wall Sit, Reverse Lunge, Glute Bridge March, Lying Heel Tap, Sit-to-Stand. Squat, hinge and core only, ten minutes, nothing overhead and nothing to hold. No push and no pull today, because both load the shoulder girdle. Save this for the next heavy carrying day.
