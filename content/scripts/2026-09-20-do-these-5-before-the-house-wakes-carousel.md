# Do these 5 before the house wakes (carousel)

- Topic: The five move morning list for the hour before anyone else is up.
- Pillar: Constraint
- Format: slideshow (8 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-09-20
- Target search phrase: quiet morning workout before the house wakes up

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Do these 5 every day this week, before the house wakes up." (situation: the
named constraint is the hour, not the difficulty. Same "do these N" cover
format as the quarter end deck, which is the series prototype.)

## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. DO THESE 5. "Do these 5 every day this week, before the house wakes up."
2. ONE. "Glute Bridge. 10 reps. Lie on your back, feet flat, push your hips up."
3. TWO. "Air Squat. 10 reps. Feet hip width, sit down slowly, stand back up tall."
4. THREE. "Knee Plank. 25 seconds. Forearms down, knees down, hold one straight line."
5. FOUR. "Wall Slide. 10 reps. Back on the wall, slide your arms up and down slowly."
6. FIVE. "Shoulder Blade Squeeze. 12 reps. Pull your shoulder blades together, then let go."
7. THE STRUCTURE. "Two rounds of the five. Ten minutes, and nothing that makes a sound."
8. YOUR TURN. "Which one will you start with?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. Poster cover, then one movement a card with the
library dose and one plain instruction sentence, so the deck is readable at
arm's length in a dark kitchen. Slides two and three carry the authored figure
(`figure: true`): Glute Bridge and Air Squat both have approved exports in
`assets/animation-library.json`.

Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library dose: Glute Bridge
(hinge, tier 1, 10 reps), Air Squat (squat, tier 3, 10 reps), Knee Plank
(core, tier 3, 25s hold), Wall Slide (pull, tier 1, wall, 10 reps),
Shoulder Blade Squeeze (pull, tier 1, 12 reps). Four patterns across five
movements; the two pull movements sit side by side because one needs a wall
and one needs nothing at all.

Slide seven is the honest slide. It names the structure and, in the footer,
says plainly what is missing and why: no push this week, because at 6am a push
is the loud one, and it comes back next week. Every footer carries the quiet
reassurance the hour actually needs.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Quiet morning workout before the house wakes up: Glute Bridge, Air Squat, Knee Plank, Wall Slide, Shoulder Blade Squeeze. Two rounds on the floor at 6am, ten minutes, and nothing that makes a sound. Save this for tomorrow morning.
