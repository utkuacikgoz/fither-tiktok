## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. DO THESE 3. "Do these 3 every day this week. Monday has no room in it."
2. ONE. "Air Squat. 10 reps. Feet hip width, sit down between your heels, stand tall."
3. TWO. "Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Hold the frame, lean back, pull your chest to your hands."
4. THREE. "Glute Bridge March. 10 reps. Hips up, then lift one knee at a time, hips still."
5. WHAT THIS SKIPS. "No push and no core this week. Both come back on the first week with room."
6. THE STRUCTURE. "Three rounds of the three. Seven minutes, and nothing left to decide."
7. YOUR TURN. "Which three get you through Monday?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. Slides two and three carry the authored figure at
its peak pose (`figure: true`), because Air Squat and Doorframe Row both have
approved exports in `assets/animation-library.json`. Glute Bridge March has no
export, so slide four stays typographic.

Slide five is the honest slide: it names what three movements do not cover and
when push and core return, so the short list reads as a decision rather than a
gap. Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library dose: Air Squat
(squat, tier 3, no equipment, 10 reps), Doorframe Row (pull, tier 3, 8 reps),
Glute Bridge March (hinge, tier 2, no equipment, 10 reps). All three are quiet
and need nothing but a floor and a doorframe.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Three exercises to do every day at home: Air Squat for squat, Doorframe Row for pull, Glute Bridge March for hinge. Monday has no room in it and the rest of the week is the same, so the same three run every day and the plan asks you nothing. Save this for the week that has no room.
