- Topic: The four move kitchen session, done in the twenty minutes dinner takes.

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Do these 4 every day this week while dinner cooks." (situation mechanism: the
named constraint is the scene she is already standing in, so the deck is a
list she can run tonight without moving rooms. Same "do these N" cover shape

## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. DO THESE 4. "Do these 4 every day this week while dinner cooks."
2. ONE. "Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Hands on the wall, lower slow, press yourself away."
3. TWO. "Wall Sit. 30 seconds. Back flat on the wall, knees bent, hold while it simmers."
4. THREE. "Standing Hip Hinge. 10 reps. Push your hips back, flat back, stand tall again."
5. FOUR. "Reverse Lunge. 6 each side. Step one foot back, drop the knee, stand back up."
6. WHAT THIS SKIPS. "No pull and no core this week. Both come back next week, away from the stove."
7. THE STRUCTURE. "Two rounds of the four. Eight minutes, and the oven is your timer."
8. YOUR TURN. "Which four would you do tonight?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. The cover runs on the poster ground. Slides two
and five carry the authored figure at its peak pose (`figure: true`), because
Wall Push-Up and Reverse Lunge both have approved exports in
`assets/animation-library.json`. Wall Sit and Standing Hip Hinge have no
export, so those two slides stay pure type.

Slide six is the honest slide: four movements cover push, squat and hinge, so
it names what is missing and when it returns instead of pretending the list is
whole. Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library dose: Wall
Push-Up (push, tier 1, wall, 10 reps), Wall Sit (squat, tier 2, wall, 30s
hold), Standing Hip Hinge (hinge, tier 1, 10 reps), Reverse Lunge (squat, tier
4, 6 reps, written here as 6 each side). Every one is quiet, so the whole
session runs with a kid asleep down the hall.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Kitchen workout while dinner cooks: four moves, eight minutes, one wall. Wall Push-Up, Wall Sit, Standing Hip Hinge and Reverse Lunge, all quiet enough for a sleeping kid in the next room. Save this for the next twenty minutes at the stove.
