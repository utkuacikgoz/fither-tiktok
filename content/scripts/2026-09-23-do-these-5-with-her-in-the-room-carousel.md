## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Do these 5 every day this week, with her in the room." (situation mechanism,
on the owner's "same moves every day of the worst week" format. The named
constraint is not the clock, it is the person on the floor with you.)

## 2. SCRIPT (9 slides)

1. DO THESE 5. "Do these 5 every day this week, with her in the room."
2. ONE. "Glute Bridge. 10 reps. On your back, knees bent, press through your heels and lift your hips."
3. TWO. "Glute Bridge March. 10 reps. Stay up at the top and raise one knee at a time."
4. THREE. "Kneeling Balance Reach. 8 reps. From all fours, stretch one arm out level with your shoulder."
5. FOUR. "Reverse Lunge. 6 reps each side. Step one foot straight back, lower your back knee, stand up."
6. FIVE. "Knee Plank. 25 seconds. Elbows under your shoulders, knees down, hold your body in one line."
7. WHAT THIS SKIPS. "No push and no pull this week. Both come back the week you get a closed door."
8. THE STRUCTURE. "Two rounds of the five. Ten minutes, floor level, nothing tips over."
9. YOUR TURN. "Where does she sit while you train?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. Slides two and five carry the authored figure
(`figure: true`), because Glute Bridge and Reverse Lunge both have approved
exports in `assets/animation-library.json`. The other three movement slides
stay text only rather than borrowing a figure that does not exist.

Slide seven is the honest slide. Five movements at floor level cannot cover
push or pull, so the deck says which patterns are missing and names the day
they return: the week a door actually closes. Every movement is in
`movement-library.md` at its library dose: Glute Bridge (hinge, tier 1, 10
reps), Glute Bridge March (hinge, tier 2, 10 reps), Kneeling Balance Reach
(core, tier 2, 8 reps), Reverse Lunge (squat, tier 4, 6 reps), Knee Plank
(core, tier 3, 25s hold).

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS
