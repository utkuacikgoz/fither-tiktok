## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Do these 3 every day this week. Decide nothing else." (the video's hook, held as
slide one so a viewer who scrolls past the video meets the same named
constraint. EXP-007 named-constraint group.)

## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. QUARTER END. "Do these 3 every day this week. Decide nothing else."
2. ONE. "Sit-to-Stand. 8 reps. Out of your desk chair with no hands, back down slowly."
3. TWO. "Doorframe Lean Row. 10 reps. Hold the frame, lean back, pull yourself in."
4. THREE. "Glute Bridge. 10 reps. Lie down by the desk, feet flat, push your hips up."
5. WHAT THIS SKIPS. "No push and no core this week. They come back when the week opens up."
6. THE STRUCTURE. "Three rounds of the three. Seven minutes, start to finish."
7. YOUR TURN. "Which three would you pick?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. The video compresses the three movements into a
spoken beat each; the carousel names them in full with the library dose and
the pattern each one covers, which is what makes it the screenshot.

Slide five is the honest slide and the reason the list is short rather than
thin: it says plainly what three movements do not cover, and when push and
core come back. Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library
dose: Sit-to-Stand (squat, tier 2, chair, 8 reps), Doorframe Lean Row (pull,
tier 2, 10 reps), Glute Bridge (hinge, tier 1, 10 reps).

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Minimum workout for a busy work week: three movements, three patterns, seven minutes. Sit-to-Stand for squat, Doorframe Lean Row for pull, Glute Bridge for hinge. The same three every day, so the plan never asks you anything. Save this for the week everything lands at once.
