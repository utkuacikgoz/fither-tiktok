## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. DO THESE 3. "Do these 3 every day this week at 6pm, after sitting all day."
2. ONE. "Standing Hip Hinge. 10 reps. Stand tall, push your hips back, then stand up again."
3. TWO. "Wall Slide. 10 reps. Back against the wall, slide your arms up and down slowly."
4. THREE. "Air Squat. 10 reps. Feet under your shoulders, down slowly, up all the way."
5. WHAT THIS SKIPS. "No push and no core this week. They come back when your evenings open up."
6. THE STRUCTURE. "Three rounds of the three. Seven minutes, laptop closed first."
7. YOUR TURN. "Which one would you start with at 6pm?"
