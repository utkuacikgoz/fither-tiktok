## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

2. ONE. "Air Squat. 10 reps. Feet hip width, sit down slowly, stand back up tall."
3. TWO. "Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Hold the doorframe, lean back, pull your chest to it."
4. THREE. "Glute Bridge. 10 reps. Lie on your back, feet flat, push your hips up."
5. FOUR. "Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Hands on the wall, lower your chest, push away."
6. FIVE. "Full Plank. 30 seconds. Forearms down, legs long, hold one straight line."
7. THE STRUCTURE. "Two rounds of the five. About ten minutes, with everyone still home."
8. YOUR TURN. "Which room will you train in?"
