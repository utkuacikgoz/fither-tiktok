## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. NO DUMBBELLS. "A strong back with no dumbbells: four floor raises, easiest to hardest."
2. RUNG ONE. "Shoulder Blade Squeeze. 12 reps. Lie face down, pull your shoulder blades together."
3. RUNG TWO. "Prone W Raise. 10 reps. Face down, elbows bent in a W, lift your arms off the floor."
4. RUNG THREE. "Prone Y Raise. 10 reps. Face down, arms long in a Y, lift them off the floor."
5. THE TOP. "Prone Y-T-W Raise. 5 reps. Face down, lift your arms in a Y, then a T, then a W."
6. HONEST NOTE. "The top rung takes weeks, not days."
7. YOUR TURN. "Which rung are you on?"

Footers on slides 2 to 4 carry the "Ready when" test for the next rung. The
top rung's footer names the capability instead: lifting the box onto the top
shelf and holding it there.

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. Every movement named is in `movement-library.md`

12 reps), Prone W Raise (pull, T2, 10 reps), Prone Y Raise (pull, T3, 10
