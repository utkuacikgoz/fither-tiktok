# Ten minutes is complete (carousel)

- Topic: The reframe that ten minutes is the session, not the warm-up for a longer one that never arrives, with a real ten minute session to prove it.
- Pillar: Reframe
- Format: slideshow (7 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-09-26
- Target search phrase: is a 10 minute workout enough

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Ten minutes is a complete session. Nobody told you that." (contradiction:
owner format #5, as written. It contradicts the belief that a real workout
is an hour, and the body slides resolve it with a session she can run today.)

## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. NOBODY TOLD YOU. "Ten minutes is a complete session. Nobody told you that."
2. THE MYTH. "Ten minutes is not a warm-up for a real workout that never comes."
3. THE MATH. "Squat, pull, hinge, push, core. All five patterns fit in ten minutes."
4. THE TRUTH. "The ten minutes you do beats the hour you plan."
5. WHAT YOU BUILD. "Ten minutes today and ten tomorrow is strength you build and keep."
6. TRY IT. "Half Squat 10, Wall Slide 10, Standing Hip Hinge 10, Incline Push-Up 8, Knee Plank 25s."
7. YOUR TURN. "When will your ten minutes be?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. Slides two to five make the case in one plain
line each; slide six is the proof, a real ten minute session in one line;
slide seven hands the timing back to her.

The TRY IT session names five movements from `movement-library.md` at their
library doses, one per pattern: Half Squat (squat, T2, 10 reps), Wall Slide
(pull, T1, wall, 10 reps), Standing Hip Hinge (hinge, T1, 10 reps), Incline
Push-Up (push, T2, chair, 8 reps), Knee Plank (core, T3, 25s hold). The week
plan's rule is that no day names the same movement in two decks, and the
day's series deck already uses Air Squat, Doorframe Row, Glute Bridge, Wall
Push-Up and Full Plank, while the ladder uses the four floor raises. So this
session takes the neighboring movement in each pattern instead. It is a
summary slide, so no figure, and none of these five has an approved export.

No line calls the session short, quick or mini; the whole deck argues the
opposite. Two capability lines: "the ten minutes is the workout" and
"strength you build and keep".

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Is a 10 minute workout enough? Ten minutes is a complete session, not a warm-up for one that never comes: Half Squat, Wall Slide, Standing Hip Hinge, Incline Push-Up, Knee Plank, two rounds. Save this for your next ten minutes.
