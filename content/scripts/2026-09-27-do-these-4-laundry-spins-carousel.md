3. TWO. "Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Hands on the wall, lower slow, press yourself away."
4. THREE. "Wall Sit. 30 seconds. Back flat on the wall, knees bent, hold through the rinse."
5. FOUR. "Doorframe Lean Row. 10 reps. Hold the doorframe, lean back, pull yourself in."

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Slide six is the honest slide: four movements cover hinge, push, squat and

whole session runs with a kid asleep down the hall. Home scene only.
