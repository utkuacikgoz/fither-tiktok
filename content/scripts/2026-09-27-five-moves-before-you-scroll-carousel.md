# Five moves before you scroll (carousel)

- Topic: Five moves she can do from, or right beside, the seat she is scrolling in, done once before she keeps scrolling.
- Pillar: Fast tips
- Format: slideshow (8 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-09-27
- Target search phrase: exercises you can do while sitting

## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. BEFORE YOU SCROLL. "You scrolled your way to five moves you can do right where you sit."
2. ONE. "Seated Knee Lift. 10 reps. Sit tall on the edge, lift one knee, then the other."
3. TWO. "Shoulder Blade Squeeze. 12 reps. Phone down, squeeze your shoulder blades, let go."
4. THREE. "Sit-to-Stand. 8 reps. Stand up from the seat with no hands, sit back down slowly."
5. FOUR. "Air Squat. 10 reps. Stand in front of the seat, sit your hips back, stand tall."
6. FIVE. "Wall Slide. 10 reps. Back to the wall beside you, slide your arms up, then down."
7. THE DEAL. "Do all five once. Then scroll." Footer: "About four minutes. Then the phone is yours."
8. YOUR TURN. "Where are you scrolling from right now?" Footer: "Save this for the next scroll."

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

The four minute estimate covers one pass of the listed reps at an easy pace.
