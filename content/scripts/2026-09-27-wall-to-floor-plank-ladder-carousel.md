## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. THE PLANK LADDER. "Week one: a plank on the wall. Week eight: a plank on the floor."
2. RUNG ONE. "Wall Plank. 20 seconds. Hands on the wall, walk your feet back, body in one line." Footer: "Ready when 20 seconds feels easy."
3. RUNG TWO. "Incline Plank. 20 seconds. Hands on a chair against the wall, body straight, hold." Footer: "Ready when your hips stay level the whole 20 seconds."
4. RUNG THREE. "Knee Plank. 25 seconds. Forearms and knees down, hips in line, hold." Footer: "Ready when 25 seconds feels steady."
5. RUNG FOUR. "Full Plank. 30 seconds. On your forearms and toes, body long, keep breathing." Footer: "Ready when 30 seconds feels steady."
6. THE TOP. "Plank Shoulder Tap. 10 reps. Up on your hands, tap one shoulder, then the other." Footer: "Ready when your hips stay still on every tap."
7. HONEST NOTE. "The wall, chair and shoulder-tap rungs load your wrists. The forearm rungs do not." Footer: "Wrists tired? Stay on the forearm rungs."
8. YOUR TURN. "Which rung are you on?" Footer: "Save the ladder."

Typography only, no footage. The cover runs on the gold poster ground. Slide

chair, 20s hold), Knee Plank (core, tier 3, 25s hold), Full Plank (core,
tier 4, 30s hold), Plank Shoulder Tap (core, tier 5, 10 reps).

Slide seven is the honest slide and it follows the library's load column
exactly: Wall Plank, Incline Plank and Plank Shoulder Tap list the wrists;
Knee Plank and Full Plank do not, so both are written as forearm holds.

note only says which rungs load the hands. The chair rung says
"against the wall" so the chair cannot slide. Home scene only.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Plank progression for beginners: five rungs from a plank on the wall to shoulder taps on the floor, each with the test that says you are ready for the next. Wall Plank, Incline Plank, Knee Plank, Full Plank, then Plank Shoulder Tap at the top. Save the ladder.
