# Do these 3, the lunch break in work clothes (carousel)

- Topic: The three move minimum that fits a lunch break at work, no floor and no change of clothes, as a saveable list.
- Pillar: Constraint
- Format: slideshow (7 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-09-28
- Target search phrase: exercises to do on your lunch break

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Do these 3 every day this week on your lunch break, in work clothes." (situation
mechanism: the named hour and the named constraint, not a promise. Same cover
shape as the do-these prototype; "in work clothes" is the second-line
reassurance that answers the changing-room objection.)

## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. DO THESE 3. "Do these 3 every day this week on your lunch break, in work clothes."
2. ONE. "Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Hands on the wall, chest toward it, push away."
3. TWO. "Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Hold the frame, lean back, pull your chest to your hands."
4. THREE. "Seated Knee Lift. 10 reps. Sit tall on the chair, lift one knee at a time."
5. WHAT THIS SKIPS. "No squat and no hinge this week. Both come back next week."
6. THE STRUCTURE. "Three rounds of the three. About seven minutes, and no changing."
7. YOUR TURN. "Where will you do these at lunch?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. Slides two, three and four carry the authored
figure at its peak pose (`figure: true`), because Wall Push-Up, Doorframe Row
and Seated Knee Lift all have approved exports in
`assets/animation-library.json`.

Slide five is the honest slide: it names the two patterns three movements do
not cover and says when they return, so the short list reads as a decision
rather than a gap. Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library
dose: Wall Push-Up (push, tier 1, wall, 10 reps), Doorframe Row (pull, tier 3,
wall, 8 reps), Seated Knee Lift (core, tier 1, chair, 10 reps). None needs the
floor, all three are quiet, and the push is not described as easy on the
wrists because it is not.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Exercises to do on your lunch break at work: Wall Push-Up, Doorframe Row and Seated Knee Lift, three rounds in about seven minutes. A wall, a doorframe and your desk chair, and you never change your clothes. Save this for lunch.
