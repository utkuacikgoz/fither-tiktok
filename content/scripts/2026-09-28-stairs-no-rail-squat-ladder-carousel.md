## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. THE STAIRS. "If you grab the rail when your hands are full, this is for you."
2. RUNG ONE. "Partial Squat. 10 reps. Sit back a few inches, then stand tall."
3. RUNG TWO. "Half Squat. 10 reps. Sit back halfway down, then stand tall."
4. RUNG THREE. "Air Squat. 10 reps. Feet hip width, sit down between your heels, stand tall."
5. RUNG FOUR. "Paused Squat. 8 reps. Squat down, hold for two seconds, stand up."
6. THE TOP. "Split Squat. 8 reps each side. One foot forward, lower the back knee, stand up."
7. HONEST NOTE. "The top rung takes months, not a week."
8. YOUR TURN. "Which rung are you on?"

Every footer on slides 2 to 5 carries the test that says she is ready for the
next rung. The top footer names the capability: the stairs with a full
laundry basket, no hand on the rail.

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. Slide four carries the authored figure at its
peak pose (`figure: true`), because Air Squat is the only rung with an
approved export in `assets/animation-library.json`; every other rung stays
typographic. Every movement named is in `movement-library.md` in tier order
with its library dose: Partial Squat (T1, 10 reps), Half Squat (T2, 10 reps),
Air Squat (T3, 10 reps), Paused Squat (T3, 8 reps), Split Squat (T4, 8 reps).
The capability is framed as something she can do, not as a safety or fall
claim.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Exercises to make stairs easier, in five rungs from Partial Squat to Split Squat, each with the test that says you are ready for the next one. Start on the rung that feels steady and climb when the test says so. Save the ladder for the next laundry day.
