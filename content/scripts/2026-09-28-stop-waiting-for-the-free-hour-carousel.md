# Stop waiting for the free hour (carousel)

- Topic: The free hour never arrives; the ten minutes already did. A reframe that ends in a real session she can run today.
- Pillar: Reframe
- Format: slideshow (7 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-09-28
- Target search phrase: how to work out when you have no time

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Stop waiting for the free hour. Seriously. It is not coming." (contradiction
mechanism, owner format #38: it turns the usual advice, wait until you have
time, on its head. The loop it opens, then when, closes on the ten-minute
session and the question of where her ten minutes are today.)

## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. SERIOUSLY. "Stop waiting for the free hour. Seriously. It is not coming."
2. THE WAIT. "The free hour has not come since you started waiting for it."
3. THE CALENDAR. "Your calendar already has ten minutes in it. Between calls. Before pickup."
4. THE SWAP. "Ten minutes. Three moves. Done. That is a complete session."
5. TRY IT. "Sit-to-Stand, 8 reps. Wall Push-Up, 10 reps. Glute Bridge, 10 reps. Three rounds."
6. WHAT YOU KEEP. "Ten minutes today, ten tomorrow. The strength you build is yours."
7. YOUR TURN. "Where are your ten minutes today?"

The footer on slide two ("That is the calendar, not you.") keeps the case
kind: the missing hour is a fact about the day, not a failing.

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. No slide carries the figure: the only slide that
names movements names three of them, so no single approved export belongs on
it. Every movement named is in `movement-library.md` at its library dose:
Sit-to-Stand (squat, tier 2, chair, 8 reps), Wall Push-Up (push, tier 1, wall,
10 reps), Glute Bridge (hinge, tier 1, no equipment, 10 reps). Three rounds
with rests lands near ten minutes. All three are quiet.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

How to work out when you have no time: stop waiting for the free hour and use the ten minutes already in your day. Sit-to-Stand, Wall Push-Up and Glute Bridge, three rounds, done. Save this for your ten minutes.
