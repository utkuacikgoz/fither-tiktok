# Do these 5 on five hours of sleep (carousel)

- Topic: The five move list for a week of short nights, easy on purpose.
- Pillar: Constraint
- Format: slideshow (8 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-09-29
- Target search phrase: easy workout when you are tired

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Do these 5 every day this week, even on five hours of sleep." (situation: the
named constraint is the short night, not the difficulty. Same "do these N"
cover format as the quarter end deck, which is the series prototype.)

## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. DO THESE 5. "Do these 5 every day this week, even on five hours of sleep."
2. ONE. "Glute Bridge. 10 reps. Lie on your back, feet flat, push your hips up."
3. TWO. "Lying Heel Slide. 10 reps. On your back, knees bent, slide one heel out and back."
4. THREE. "Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Hands on the wall, lower your chest, press back."
5. FOUR. "Supported Sit-to-Stand. 8 reps. Use your hands to help you up. Sit back down slowly."
6. FIVE. "Seated Knee Lift. 10 reps. Sit tall on the chair, lift one knee, then the other."
7. THE STRUCTURE. "One or two rounds, slow. About eight minutes. On five hours, that counts."
8. YOUR TURN. "How many hours did you get last night?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. Poster cover, then one movement a card with the
library dose and one plain instruction sentence. Slides two, four and six
carry the authored figure (`figure: true`): Glute Bridge, Wall Push-Up and
Seated Knee Lift all have approved exports in `assets/animation-library.json`.
Lying Heel Slide and Supported Sit-to-Stand have none, so they stay type only.

Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library dose: Glute Bridge
(hinge, tier 1, 10 reps), Lying Heel Slide (core, tier 1, 10 reps), Wall
Push-Up (push, tier 1, wall, 10 reps), Supported Sit-to-Stand (squat, tier 1,
chair, 8 reps), Seated Knee Lift (core, tier 1, chair, 10 reps). All tier 1.
The order runs floor, floor, wall, chair, chair, so she gets up once and never
goes back down.

Every movement footer says "easy on purpose" out loud, because on five hours
the permission is the point. Slide seven is the honest slide: one or two slow
rounds, about eight minutes, and the footer says plainly that pull is skipped
this week and comes back when she has slept. No claim about sleep, energy or
recovery anywhere in the deck.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Easy workout when you are tired: Glute Bridge, Lying Heel Slide, Wall Push-Up, Supported Sit-to-Stand, Seated Knee Lift. Easy on purpose, one or two slow rounds, about eight minutes on five hours of sleep. Save this for the next short night.
