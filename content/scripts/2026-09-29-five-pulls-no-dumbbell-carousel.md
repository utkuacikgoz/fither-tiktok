## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. NO DUMBBELL. "Five ways to train a pull without a single dumbbell."
2. ONE. "Shoulder Blade Squeeze. 12 reps. Pull your shoulder blades together, then let go."
3. TWO. "Wall Slide. 10 reps. Back on the wall, slide your arms up and down slowly."
4. THREE. "Doorframe Lean Row. 10 reps. Hold the frame, lean back a little, pull yourself in."
5. FOUR. "Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Walk your feet closer, lean back more, pull your chest in."
6. FIVE. "Prone Y Raise. 10 reps. Lie face down, arms in a Y, lift them off the floor."
7. PICK ONE. "Start with the first one that feels hard by the last rep. That is your level."
8. YOUR TURN. "Which one felt hard first?"
