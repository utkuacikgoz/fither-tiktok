## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. DO THESE 4. "Do these 4 every day this week in the ten minutes before pickup."
2. ONE. "Reverse Lunge. 6 reps each side. Step one foot back, lower the knee, stand up."
3. TWO. "Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Hold the frame, lean back, pull your chest to it."
4. THREE. "Seated Knee Lift. 10 reps. Sit tall, lift one knee, lower slow, switch."
5. FOUR. "Standing Hip Hinge. 10 reps. Push your hips back, flat back, stand tall again."
6. WHAT THIS SKIPS. "No push this week. It comes back next week, at the wall."
7. THE STRUCTURE. "Two rounds of the four. About eight minutes, keys by the door."
8. YOUR TURN. "Which move do you start with?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. The cover runs on the poster ground. Slides two,
three and four carry the authored figure at its peak pose (`figure: true`),
because Reverse Lunge, Doorframe Row and Seated Knee Lift all have approved
exports in `assets/animation-library.json`. Standing Hip Hinge has no export,
so slide five stays pure type.

Slide six is the honest slide: four movements cover squat, pull, core and
hinge, so it names the missing push and when it returns instead of pretending
the list is whole. Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library
dose: Reverse Lunge (squat, tier 4, 6 reps, written here as 6 each side),
Doorframe Row (pull, tier 3, wall, 8 reps), Seated Knee Lift (core, tier 1,
chair, 10 reps), Standing Hip Hinge (hinge, tier 1, 10 reps). Every one is
quiet, so the session runs with a nap still going down the hall. Home scene
only: hallway, doorway, kitchen chair, front door.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS
