# One leg bridge ladder (carousel)

- Topic: The five rung hinge ladder from a two leg glute bridge on the floor to a single leg bridge with the heel on a chair.
- Pillar: Skill ladder
- Format: slideshow (8 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-09-30
- Target search phrase: glute bridge progression

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"A glute bridge looks like nothing. Five rungs later it is on one leg."
(contradiction mechanism, built on the owner's format #43 "surprising
effectiveness": the cover sets the easiest move she knows against where it
ends up, and the five rungs resolve the gap.)

## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. THE BRIDGE LADDER. "A glute bridge looks like nothing. Five rungs later it is on one leg."
2. RUNG ONE. "Glute Bridge. 10 reps. Feet flat on the floor, lift your hips, lower slow."
3. RUNG TWO. "Paused Glute Bridge. 8 reps. Lift your hips, hold two seconds, lower slow."
4. RUNG THREE. "Feet-Elevated Glute Bridge. 10 reps. Heels on a chair, lift your hips, lower."
5. RUNG FOUR. "Single-Leg Glute Bridge. 8 reps each side. One foot down, other knee up, lift."
6. THE TOP. "Single-Leg Elevated Bridge. 6 reps each side. One heel on the chair, lift."
7. HONEST NOTE. "The top rung takes months, not a week."
8. YOUR TURN. "Which rung are you on?"

Every footer on slides 2 to 6 is a "Ready when ..." test: on the rungs it says
when to move up, and on the top it says when she owns it.
