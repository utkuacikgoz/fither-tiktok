## 2. SCRIPT (6 slides)

1. AT 35. "Three things I would tell myself before I started strength training at 35."
2. ONE. "Ten minutes counts as a full session. It is not a warm-up for later."
3. TWO. "Start at the wall, not the floor. Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Lower slow, press away."
4. THREE. "The same few moves every day beat a new plan every Monday."
5. START HERE. "Wall Push-Up, 10 reps. Sit-to-Stand, 8 reps. Glute Bridge, 10 reps."
6. YOUR TURN. "What would you tell yourself?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. The cover runs on the poster ground. Slide three

the tips are about time, starting point and repetition, not about what
happens to a body at 35. Home scene only: a wall, a chair, the floor.
