## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. DO THESE 4. "Do these 4 every day this week, even when it rains."
2. ONE. "Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Hands on the wall, lower slow, press yourself away."
3. TWO. "Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Hold the frame, lean back, pull your chest to the door."
4. THREE. "Glute Bridge. 10 reps. Lie on your back, feet flat, lift your hips, lower slow."
5. FOUR. "Knee Plank. 25 seconds. Forearms and knees down, body in one line, breathe."
6. WHAT THIS SKIPS. "No squat this week. It comes back next week, with a wall and a chair."
7. THE STRUCTURE. "Three rounds of the four. About ten minutes, and you never leave the house."
8. YOUR TURN. "Which room are you training in today?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. The cover runs on the poster ground. Slides two,
three and four carry the authored figure at its peak pose (`figure: true`),
because Wall Push-Up, Doorframe Row and Glute Bridge all have approved exports
in `assets/animation-library.json`. Knee Plank has no export, so that slide
stays pure type.

Slide six is the honest slide: four movements cover push, pull, hinge and
core, so it names the missing squat and when it returns instead of pretending
the list is whole. Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library
dose: Wall Push-Up (push, tier 1, wall, 10 reps), Doorframe Row (pull, tier 3,
wall, 8 reps), Glute Bridge (hinge, tier 1, no equipment, 10 reps), Knee Plank
(core, tier 3, no equipment, 25s hold). Every one is quiet, so the whole
session runs with a kid asleep down the hall. No movement here repeats one
from the floor test posted the same day.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Rainy day workout at home: four moves, about ten minutes, nothing to buy. Wall Push-Up, Doorframe Row, Glute Bridge and Knee Plank, all quiet and all indoors. Save this for the rainy week.
