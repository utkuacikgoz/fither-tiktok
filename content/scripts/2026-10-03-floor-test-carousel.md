# The floor test (carousel)

- Topic: A self-test she can take on the spot, then the one move that matches her result.
- Pillar: Skill ladder
- Series: the-test (first deck; owner picked this hook 2026-10-03)
- Format: slideshow (8 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-10-03
- Target search phrase: how to get off the floor without using your hands

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Can you stand up from the floor without your hands? Try it, then swipe." (format #79, the test. The cover asks her to do something in
the next ten seconds; the CTA asks for her result, which invites comments.)

## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. THE FLOOR TEST. "Can you stand up from the floor without your hands? Try it, then swipe."
2. HOW TO TEST. "Sit on the floor, legs crossed. Stand up without your hands, a knee or the couch."
3. BOTH HANDS. "Start here. Supported Sit-to-Stand. 8 reps. Hands on the chair, stand, sit down slowly."
4. ONE HAND. "Sit-to-Stand. 8 reps. Arms folded, stand up from the chair, sit down slowly."
5. A KNEE. "Reverse Lunge. 6 each side. Step one foot back, lower the knee, stand up."
6. NOTHING. "Single-Leg Sit-to-Stand. 4 reps. One leg, no hands, up from the chair."
7. RETEST. "Do your level every day. Take the floor test again in two weeks."
8. YOUR TURN. "What did you need: both hands, one hand, a knee, or nothing?"

Slides 3 to 6 are the results, easiest to hardest. Each footer says when to
move on, so the deck is a test and a ladder at once.

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

No claim about health, falls or longevity: the test is a starting point, not

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Can you get up off the floor without using your hands? Take the floor test, then find your level: Supported Sit-to-Stand, Sit-to-Stand, Reverse Lunge or Single-Leg Sit-to-Stand. Do your level every day and test again in two weeks. Save this and retest.
