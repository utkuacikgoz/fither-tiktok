## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. DO THESE 4. "Do these 4 every day this week while the coffee brews."
2. ONE. "Air Squat. 10 reps. Feet hip width, sit back and down, stand up tall."
3. TWO. "Standing Hip Hinge. 10 reps. Push your hips back, flat back, stand tall again."
4. THREE. "Doorframe Lean Row. 10 reps. Hold the frame, lean back, pull yourself in."
5. FOUR. "Seated Knee Lift. 10 reps. Sit tall on a chair, lift one knee, lower it slowly."
6. WHAT THIS SKIPS. "No push this week. It comes back next week, at the kitchen wall."
7. THE STRUCTURE. "Two rounds of the four. About six minutes, done before the last drip."
8. YOUR TURN. "How long does your coffee take?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. The cover runs on the gold poster ground. Slides
two and five carry the authored figure at its peak pose (`figure: true`),
because Air Squat and Seated Knee Lift both have approved exports in
`assets/animation-library.json`. Standing Hip Hinge and Doorframe Lean Row
have no export, so those slides stay pure type (Doorframe Row is approved,
Doorframe Lean Row is not, so no figure there).

Slide six is the honest slide: the four moves cover squat, hinge, pull and
core, so it names push as the missing pattern and says when it returns. Every
movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library dose: Air Squat (squat,
tier 3, no equipment, 10 reps), Standing Hip Hinge (hinge, tier 1, no
equipment, 10 reps), Doorframe Lean Row (pull, tier 2, wall, 10 reps), Seated
Knee Lift (core, tier 1, chair, 10 reps). Every one is quiet, so the session
runs while the rest of the house sleeps.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS
