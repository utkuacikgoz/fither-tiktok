- Topic: A wall push-up count she can take on the spot, then the one move that matches her number.
- Pillar: Skill ladder

## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. THE PUSH-UP TEST. "How many wall push-ups can you do in a row? Try it, then swipe."
2. HOW TO TEST. "Hands on the wall at chest height. Count clean reps until you stop."
3. UNDER 5. "Start here. Wall Push-Up Hold. 20 seconds. Lower halfway to the wall and hold still."
4. 5 TO 9. "Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Hands on the wall, lower slow, press yourself away."
5. 10 TO 19. "Incline Push-Up. 8 reps. Hands on a sturdy chair, lower your chest, press up."
6. 20 OR MORE. "Kneeling Push-Up. 8 reps. Knees down, body straight, lower your chest, press up."
7. RETEST. "Do your level every day. Take the wall test again in two weeks."
8. YOUR TURN. "How many did you get?"

Slides 3 to 6 are the results, easiest to hardest. Each footer says when to
move on, so the deck is a test and a ladder at once. The HOW TO TEST footer
carries the honest line: every push-up loads the wrists. Nothing in the deck
claims otherwise.

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, gold poster cover. The figure appears on the Wall Push-Up

begin and when to move on, nothing more.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

How many wall push-ups can you do in a row? Take the push-up test, then find your level: Wall Push-Up Hold, Wall Push-Up, Incline Push-Up or Kneeling Push-Up. Save this and retest in two weeks.
