- Topic: Four no-equipment moves to do for two weeks before spending money, and an honest line on when buying makes sense.
- Pillar: Fast tips
- Format: slideshow (7 slides, TikTok photo post)

## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. BEFORE YOU BUY. "Before you buy dumbbells, do these 4 for two weeks."
2. ONE. "Reverse Lunge. 6 each side. Step one foot back, lower the knee, stand up."
3. TWO. "Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Hold the frame, lean back, pull your chest in."
4. THREE. "Glute Bridge. 10 reps. Lie on your back, feet flat, push your hips up."
5. FOUR. "Prone Y Raise. 10 reps. Lie face down, arms in a Y, lift them off the floor."
6. WHEN TO BUY. "When all four feel easy every day for two weeks, buying dumbbells makes sense."
7. YOUR TURN. "Which one felt hardest today?"

The WHEN TO BUY slide is the honest one: it does not say never buy. It gives
her a clear test, and the footer defines "easy" so she can judge it herself.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Home workout without dumbbells: Reverse Lunge, Doorframe Row, Glute Bridge and Prone Y Raise, every day for two weeks. When all four feel easy, buying dumbbells makes sense. Save this before you buy anything.
