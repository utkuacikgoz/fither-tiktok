## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. DO THESE 5. "Do these 5 every day this week, between video calls."
2. ONE. "Sit-to-Stand. 8 reps. Arms folded, stand up from your chair, sit down slowly."
3. TWO. "Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Hands on the wall, lower your chest, press back."
4. THREE. "Seated Knee Lift. 10 reps. Sit tall on the chair, lift one knee, then the other."
5. FOUR. "Shoulder Blade Squeeze. 12 reps. Pull your shoulder blades together, then let go."
6. FIVE. "Standing Hip Hinge. 10 reps. Push your hips back, keep your back flat, stand tall."
7. THE STRUCTURE. "One round in each gap, about three minutes. Three gaps make a full session."
8. YOUR TURN. "How many calls do you have today?"

Five-move deck, so there is no separate WHAT THIS SKIPS slide: the structure
footer carries the skip note, and this week it says nothing is skipped,
because squat, push, core, pull and hinge are all here.

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, gold poster cover. Slides three and four carry the authored
figure (`figure: true`): Wall Push-Up and Seated Knee Lift both have approved
exports in `assets/animation-library.json`. Sit-to-Stand, Shoulder Blade
Squeeze and Standing Hip Hinge have none, so they stay type only.

Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library dose: Sit-to-Stand
(squat, tier 2, chair, 8 reps), Wall Push-Up (push, tier 1, wall, 10 reps),
Seated Knee Lift (core, tier 1, chair, 10 reps), Shoulder Blade Squeeze (pull,
tier 1, 12 reps), Standing Hip Hinge (hinge, tier 1, 10 reps). Everything
happens at the desk: chair, the wall beside it, chair again, then standing.
No floor work, so she never changes out of work clothes.

The Sit-to-Stand footer is a practical note about a rolling desk chair, not a
claim. Capability lines live in the footers ("You never leave your seat",
"Done before the next call starts") and the structure slide, which tells her
three short gaps add up to a full session.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Workout between meetings at home: Sit-to-Stand, Wall Push-Up, Seated Knee Lift, Shoulder Blade Squeeze, Standing Hip Hinge. One quiet round in each gap between video calls, about three minutes, all five patterns covered. Save this for your next gap between calls.
