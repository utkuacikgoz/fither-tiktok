## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. THE WALL SIT TEST. "How long can you hold a wall sit? Try it, then swipe."
2. HOW TO TEST. "Back flat on the wall. Slide down until your thighs are level. Count the seconds."
3. UNDER 15 SECONDS. "Start here. High Wall Sit. 20 seconds. Back on the wall, slide down only a little."
4. 15 TO 29. "Wall Sit. 30 seconds. Back flat on the wall, thighs level, hold."
5. 30 TO 59. "Paused Squat. 8 reps. Squat down, hold two seconds at the bottom, stand up."
6. 60 OR MORE. "Split Squat. 8 each side. One foot forward, one back, lower straight down, stand up."
7. RETEST. "Do your level every day. Take the wall sit test again in two weeks."
8. YOUR TURN. "How many seconds did you get?"

Slides 3 to 6 are the results, easiest to hardest. Each footer says when to
move on, so the deck is a test and a ladder at once.
