## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. DO THESE 3. "Do these 3 every day this week, during nap time."
2. ONE. "Glute Bridge. 10 reps. Lie on your back, feet flat, lift your hips, lower slowly."
3. TWO. "Doorframe Lean Row. 10 reps. Hold the frame, lean back a little, pull yourself in."
4. THREE. "Wall Sit. 30 seconds. Back flat on the wall, slide down until your knees bend, hold."
5. WHAT THIS SKIPS. "No push and no core this week. Both come back next week."
6. THE STRUCTURE. "Three rounds of the three. About seven minutes, well inside one nap."
7. YOUR TURN. "Which room will you use during nap time?"

The footers carry the quiet: each move slide names its pattern and why it
makes no noise, and the nursery door is the one doorframe she leaves alone.
"You build this while she sleeps." is the capability line.

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, gold poster cover. The figure appears on the Glute Bridge
slide only, the one approved export in the deck. Doorframe Lean Row and Wall
Sit have no approved export, so their slides stay type only.

Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library dose: Glute Bridge
(hinge, tier 1, no equipment, 10 reps), Doorframe Lean Row (pull, tier 2,
wall, 10 reps), Wall Sit (squat, tier 2, wall, 30 second hold). All three are
quiet, which is a library-wide fact. Slide five names the two missing
patterns and says when they return, so the short list reads as a decision
rather than a gap.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Quiet exercises to do during nap time: Glute Bridge, Doorframe Lean Row and Wall Sit, three rounds in about seven minutes. No jumping and nothing to drop, so the house stays quiet. Save this for nap time.
