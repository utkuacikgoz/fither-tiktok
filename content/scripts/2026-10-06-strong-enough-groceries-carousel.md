## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. STRONG ENOUGH TO. "Strong enough to carry every grocery bag in one trip. The 4 moves."
2. ONE. "Standing Hip Hinge. 10 reps. Soft knees, push your hips back, then stand tall."
3. TWO. "Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Hold the frame, lean back, pull your chest to your hands."
4. THREE. "Air Squat. 10 reps. Feet hip width apart, sit your hips down, stand up tall."
5. FOUR. "Knee Side Plank. 20 seconds each side. Forearm and knee down, lift your hips, hold."
6. HONEST NOTE. "This takes weeks, not days. Three rounds of the four, most days. About ten minutes."
7. YOUR TURN. "How many trips does it take you now?"

Each move footer names the part of the trip it builds: the pickup, the grip,
the low lift out of the car, the one-sided carry. "You build it at home. You
use it at the car." is the capability line, and the CTA asks for a number she
already knows.
