- Topic: The free hallway version to run for two weeks before buying a gym membership, and an honest line on when buying makes sense.

"Before you pay for a gym, try this in your hallway." (contradiction

## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. BEFORE YOU BUY. "Before you pay for a gym, try this in your hallway."
2. ONE. "Reverse Lunge. 6 each side. Step one foot back, lower the knee, stand back up."
3. TWO. "Wall Sit. 30 seconds. Back flat on the hallway wall, knees bent, hold."
4. THREE. "Standing Hip Hinge. 10 reps. Push your hips back, flat back, stand tall again."
5. FOUR. "Knee Plank. 25 seconds. Forearms down, knees down, body in one straight line."
6. WHEN TO BUY. "Do three rounds every day. When all four feel easy for two weeks, a gym makes sense."
7. YOUR TURN. "Which of the four felt hardest?"

Slide six is the honest slide: it does not say never buy. It names the point
where a gym earns its cost, and the footer ("Not before. Until then, you are
building it in your hallway.") keeps her as the one doing the building.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Home workout before you join a gym: four moves in your hallway, three rounds, about ten minutes. Reverse Lunge, Wall Sit, Standing Hip Hinge and Knee Plank, every day for two weeks. Save this before you sign up for anything.
