## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. THE DOORFRAME TEST. "Can you lean back from a doorframe and pull yourself in 8 times? Try it, then swipe."
2. HOW TO TEST. "Hold both sides of a solid frame, feet close. Lean back, pull your chest in, count."
3. COULD NOT LEAN BACK. "Start here. Shoulder Blade Squeeze. 12 reps. Draw your shoulder blades together, let go."
4. A SMALL LEAN. "Doorframe Lean Row. 10 reps. Feet near the frame, lean back a little, pull yourself in."
5. 8 WITH A BIG LEAN. "Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Walk your feet closer, lean back more, pull your chest in."
6. EASY. "Deep Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Feet closer still, a deeper lean, slow on the way back."
7. RETEST. "Do your level every day. Take the doorframe test again in two weeks."
8. YOUR TURN. "How far could you lean?"

Slides 3 to 6 are the results, easiest to hardest. Each footer says when to
move on, so the deck is a test and a ladder at once. A test is a starting
point, not a score.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Back workout with a doorframe: lean back, pull yourself in, and count to 8. Take the doorframe test, then find your level: Shoulder Blade Squeeze, Doorframe Lean Row, Doorframe Row or Deep Doorframe Row. Save this and retest in two weeks.
