## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. DO THESE 5. "Do these 5 every day this week, on a work-from-home day."
2. ONE. "Air Squat. 10 reps. Feet shoulder width, sit your hips back and down, stand tall."
3. TWO. "Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Hold the frame, feet close, lean back, pull yourself in."
4. THREE. "Incline Push-Up. 8 reps. Chair against the wall, hands on the seat, lower and press."
5. FOUR. "Seated Knee Lift. 10 reps. Sit tall on the chair, lift one knee, then the other."
6. FIVE. "Hinge and Reach. 10 reps. Soft knees, push your hips back, reach forward, stand tall."
7. THE STRUCTURE. "Three rounds, about ten minutes. You trained between calls."
8. YOUR TURN. "What time is your first call?"

Five move deck, so there is no WHAT THIS SKIPS slide: the structure footer
says plainly that all five patterns are in and nothing is skipped.

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, no footage. Gold poster cover, then one movement a card with
the library dose and one plain instruction sentence. Slides two, three and
five carry the authored figure (`figure: true`): Air Squat, Doorframe Row and
Seated Knee Lift all have approved exports in `assets/animation-library.json`.
Incline Push-Up and Hinge and Reach have none, so they stay type only.

Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library dose: Air Squat
(squat, tier 3, 10 reps), Doorframe Row (pull, tier 3, wall, 8 reps), Incline
Push-Up (push, tier 2, chair, 8 reps), Seated Knee Lift (core, tier 1, chair,
10 reps), Hinge and Reach (hinge, tier 2, 10 reps). One move per pattern.

The push footer is a practical note, not a claim: a rolling desk chair is the
wrong chair for an incline push-up, so it says use one that does not roll and
the instruction puts it against the wall. Nothing in the deck calls the push
easy on the wrists. Ownership lines: "You trained between calls." and the
cover footer.
