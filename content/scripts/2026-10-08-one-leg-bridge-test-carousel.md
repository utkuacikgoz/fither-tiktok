## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. THE BRIDGE TEST. "Can you hold a one-leg bridge for 20 seconds? Try it, then swipe."
2. HOW TO TEST. "On your back, one foot down, other knee up. Lift your hips. Count the seconds each side."
3. COULD NOT LIFT. "Start here. Glute Bridge. 10 reps. Both feet down, push your hips up, lower slowly."
4. UNDER 10 SECONDS. "Paused Glute Bridge. 8 reps. Hips up, hold for three seconds, lower slowly."
5. 10 TO 19. "Single-Leg Glute Bridge. 8 each side. One foot down, other knee up, lift and lower."
6. 20 BOTH SIDES. "Single-Leg Elevated Bridge. 6 each side. One heel on a chair, lift your hips."
7. RETEST. "Do your level every day. Take the bridge test again in two weeks."
8. YOUR TURN. "How many seconds, left and right?"

Slides 3 to 6 are the results, easiest to hardest. Each footer says when to
move on, so the deck is a test and a ladder at once. The how-to footer says
to stop the count when the hips dip and to write down both sides.

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, gold poster cover. The figure appears on the Glute Bridge

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Single leg glute bridge test: lift one foot, lift your hips and count how long you hold each side. Then do your level every day, from Glute Bridge up to Single-Leg Elevated Bridge, and test again in two weeks. Save this and retest.
