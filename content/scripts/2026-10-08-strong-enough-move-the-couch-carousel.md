# Strong enough to move the couch yourself (carousel)

- Topic: A named capability from her week, and the four moves that build each part of it.
- Pillar: Reframe
- Series: none (strong enough to format)
- Format: slideshow (7 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-10-08
- Target search phrase: strong enough to move furniture on your own

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Strong enough to move the couch yourself. Start here." (format #87, strong
enough to. The situation is a Saturday at home and a room she wants to
change. The goal is a capability she can picture, never a look.)

## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. STRONG ENOUGH TO. "Strong enough to move the couch yourself. Start here."
2. ONE. "Wall Sit. 30 second hold. Back flat on the wall, slide down, hold and breathe."
3. TWO. "Reverse Lunge. 6 each side. Step one foot back, lower the knee, stand up."
4. THREE. "Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Hands on the wall, lower your chest, press back."
5. FOUR. "Full Plank. 30 second hold. On your forearms and toes, body in one straight line."
6. HONEST NOTE. "This takes weeks, not days. Then one Saturday you push it across the room yourself."
7. YOUR TURN. "What would you move first?"

Each movement footer names the part of the task it builds: the legs that
drive, the step behind the push, the push itself, and holding it all in line.
Ownership lines: the honest note's payoff ("you push it across the room
yourself") and the cover itself.

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, gold poster cover, home setting throughout. Figures appear
on the Reverse Lunge, Wall Push-Up and Full Plank slides, all approved exports
in `assets/animation-library.json`. Wall Sit has none, so it stays type only.

Every movement is in `movement-library.md` at its library dose: Wall Sit
(squat, T2, wall, 30s hold), Reverse Lunge (squat, T4, 6 each side), Wall
Push-Up (push, T1, wall, 10 reps), Full Plank (core, T4, 30s hold). The plank
is cued on the forearms, as the library loads it on core and shoulders. No
claim about backs, lifting safety or any condition; the honest note says the
timeline plainly: weeks, not days.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Strong enough to move furniture on your own, with four moves and no equipment: Wall Sit, Reverse Lunge, Wall Push-Up and Full Plank. Two rounds a day, about ten minutes, and it takes weeks, not days. Save this for the day you rearrange the room.
