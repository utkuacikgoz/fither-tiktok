## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. BEFORE YOU BUY. "Before you buy resistance bands, your doorframe already does this."
2. ONE. "Shoulder Blade Squeeze. 12 reps. Pull your shoulder blades together, then let go."
3. TWO. "Doorframe Lean Row. 10 reps. Hold the frame, lean back a little, pull yourself in."
4. THREE. "Doorframe Row. 8 reps. Walk your feet closer, lean back more, pull your chest in."
5. FOUR. "Prone Y Raise. 10 reps. Lie face down, arms in a Y, lift them off the floor."
6. WHEN TO BUY. "When all four feel easy for two weeks, a band gives you somewhere new to go."
7. YOUR TURN. "Which of the four felt hard first?"

The footers on slides 3 and 4 carry the payoff: the angle of the lean is the
band, small lean for a light one, deeper lean for a heavier one. Slide 6 is
honest that bands have a place, and says exactly when.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Back workout without resistance bands: Shoulder Blade Squeeze, Doorframe Lean Row, Doorframe Row and Prone Y Raise. Lean back further to make it harder, and buy a band when all four feel easy for two weeks. Save this before you buy.
