## 2. SCRIPT (7 slides)

1. DO THESE 3. "Do these 3 every day this week while the bath fills."
2. ONE. "Wall Sit. 30 second hold. Back flat on the wall, knees bent, hold still."
3. TWO. "Wall Slide. 10 reps. Back on the wall, slide your arms up and down slowly."
4. THREE. "Standing Hip Hinge. 10 reps. Push your hips back, keep your back flat, stand tall."
5. WHAT THIS SKIPS. "No push and no core this week. Both come back next week."
6. THE STRUCTURE. "Three rounds of the three. About seven minutes, done before the bath is full."
7. YOUR TURN. "Which of the three felt hardest?"

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Standing Hip Hinge (hinge, tier 1, none, 10 reps). All three are done

The structure footer is the capability-ownership line.
