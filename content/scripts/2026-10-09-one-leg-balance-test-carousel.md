# The balance test (carousel)

- Topic: A one-leg standing test she can take on the spot, then the one move that matches her result.
- Pillar: Skill ladder
- Series: the-test
- Format: slideshow (8 slides, TikTok photo post)
- Posting date: 2026-10-09
- Target search phrase: stand on one leg for 30 seconds

## 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)

"Can you stand on one leg for 30 seconds? Try it, then swipe." (situation
mechanism, the test shape from the floor test deck. The cover asks her to do
something in the next thirty seconds; the CTA asks for her two numbers, which
invites comments.)

## 2. SCRIPT (8 slides)

1. THE BALANCE TEST. "Can you stand on one leg for 30 seconds? Try it, then swipe."
2. HOW TO TEST. "Stand near a wall, eyes open. Lift one foot and count the seconds. Then switch sides."
3. UNDER 10 SECONDS. "Start here. Kneeling Balance Reach. 8 reps. On all fours, reach opposite arm and leg."
4. 10 TO 29. "Reverse Lunge. 6 each side. Step one foot back, lower the knee, stand up."
5. 30 BOTH SIDES. "Single-Leg Hip Hinge. 6 each side. Stand on one leg, hinge forward, stand tall."
6. EASY. "30 felt easy? Split Squat. 8 each side. Feet staggered, lower down, push back up."
7. RETEST. "Do your level every day. Take the balance test again in two weeks."
8. YOUR TURN. "How many seconds, left and right?"

Slides 3 to 6 are the results, easiest to hardest. Each footer says when to
move on, so the deck is a test and a ladder at once. The HOW TO TEST footer
tells her to use her lower side, so a split result still lands on one slide.

## 3. VISUALS / SCENE BREAKDOWN

Typography only, gold poster cover. The figure appears on the Reverse Lunge
slide, the only approved export in the deck. Every movement is in
`movement-library.md` at its library dose: Kneeling Balance Reach (core, T2,
8), Reverse Lunge (squat, T4, 6), Single-Leg Hip Hinge (hinge, T4, 6), Split
Squat (squat, T4, 8). The EASY footer points to the back-foot-on-a-chair
version without naming a new move.

This is a strength test and nothing else. No claim about falls, aging or
any health outcome appears in the slides, the caption or these notes; the
result is a starting point, not a score.

## 4. CAPTION AND HASHTAGS

Can you stand on one leg for 30 seconds? Take the balance test, then do your level every day for two weeks: Kneeling Balance Reach, Reverse Lunge, Single-Leg Hip Hinge or Split Squat. Save this and retest.
