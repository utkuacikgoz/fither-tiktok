## 2. SCRIPT (6 slides)

1. INSTEAD OF. "Instead of crunches, do these 3."
2. ONE. "Knee Plank. 25 seconds. Forearms and knees down, body in one line, hold."
3. TWO. "Seated Knee Lift. 10 reps. Sit tall on a chair, lift one knee, then the other."
4. THREE. "Lying Heel Tap. 10 reps. On your back, knees up, tap one heel down, then the other."
5. THE STRUCTURE. "Three rounds of the three. About seven minutes."
6. YOUR TURN. "Which one surprised you?"
