## 2. SCRIPT (6 slides)

1. START HERE. "Start here if push-ups feel impossible."
2. ONE. "Wall Push-Up Hold. 20 seconds. Hands on the wall, lower halfway, hold still."
3. TWO. "Wall Push-Up. 10 reps. Hands on the wall, lower slow, press away."
4. THREE. "Incline Push-Up. 8 reps. Hands on a sturdy chair against the wall, lower, press up."
5. THE STRUCTURE. "One of the three every day, the one that feels hard by the last rep."
6. YOUR TURN. "Which one is your start?"
