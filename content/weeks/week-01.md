# Week 01 — 2026-09-04 to 2026-09-10

First week on the channel. No performance data exists, so the free slot goes
to Constraint (3 Constraint, 2 Skill ladder, 1 Reframe, 1 Fast tips).
`assets/animation-library.json` has no approved exports, so every video this week is
environment POV or text on screen. Calendar context: back-to-school week,
and business travel restarting after summer.

---

## 1 — Fri 2026-09-04 — The hotel room that is enough

- **Topic**: A silent 10 minute full strength session using only a hotel
  room wall and doorframe, between landing and dinner.
- **Pillar**: Constraint
- **Why now**: business travel resumes in September; travel days read as
  lost training days, which is the exact pain FITHER exists for.
- **Angle**: the room you think has nothing in it has everything you need.
  Tension: no gym, no gear, no time, still a complete session.
- **Target search phrase**: quiet hotel room workout
- **Format**: environment POV (hotel b-roll: door, wall, window, desk)
- **Movements/patterns used**: Wall Push-Up, Doorframe Lean Row, Wall Sit,
  Glute Bridge, Wall Plank (one movement per pattern, all wall/none)
- **Assets needed**: hotel room b-roll (5 to 6 clips), voiceover. No
  animations required.
- **Reuse**: App Store screenshot "Hotel room. Ten minutes. Done."

## 2 — Sat 2026-09-05 — Wall to floor in four steps

- **Topic**: The four step ladder from wall push up to your first full push
  up, and why the wall is step one, not a cop out.
- **Pillar**: Skill ladder
- **Why now**: push up progression is evergreen search; establishes the
  channel's core promise (capability, named steps) in week one.
- **Angle**: nobody fails at push ups, they start on the wrong rung.
  Tension: the move everyone thinks they can't do has a visible staircase.
- **Target search phrase**: push up progression for beginners
- **Format**: text on screen over environment shots (wall, chair, mat)
- **Movements/patterns used**: Wall Push-Up (tier 1), Incline Push-Up
  (tier 2), Kneeling Push-Up (tier 3), Full Push-Up (tier 4)
- **Assets needed**: wall/chair/mat b-roll, voiceover. Animation of the
  four movements would upgrade this later; not required.
- **Reuse**: share card "First Full Push-Up"; onboarding progression copy.

## 3 — Sun 2026-09-06 — Strength while the oven does its job

- **Movements/patterns used**: Sit-to-Stand, Incline Push-Up, Hinge and

- **Reuse**: App Store screenshot "Kitchen mode"; adaptation explainer copy.

## 4 — Mon 2026-09-07 — Train to carry

- **Topic**: Stop training to shrink. Train to carry: the case for strength
  as the thing that makes your actual week lighter.
- **Pillar**: Reframe
- **Why now**: plants the channel's flag early. Every later video inherits
  this frame; it is also the paywall headline test.
- **Angle**: fitness content tells women to get smaller; life keeps asking
  them to be stronger. Tension: shrink vs carry.
- **Target search phrase**: strength training for busy women
- **Format**: text on screen (kinetic type over slow moving shots: stairs,
  a packed bag, groceries by a door)
- **Movements/patterns used**: patterns only (push, pull, squat, hinge,
  core), no specific exercises needed.
- **Assets needed**: 3 to 4 slow b-roll shots, voiceover.
- **Reuse**: paywall headline candidate; App Store subtitle candidate.

## 5 — Tue 2026-09-08 — The ladder no one does at home

- **Topic**: The doorframe row ladder: from a lean to a single arm row,
  the pull strength home workouts always skip.
- **Pillar**: Skill ladder
- **Why now**: differentiator. Every no-equipment account does push and
  squat; a complete pull ladder using a doorframe is rare and searchable.
- **Angle**: home workouts skip pulling because there is nothing to pull,
  except every home has doorframes. Tension: the missing half of strength.
- **Target search phrase**: back exercises at home no equipment
- **Format**: environment POV (a doorframe, shot from inside the room)
- **Movements/patterns used**: Doorframe Lean Row (tier 2), Doorframe Row
  (tier 3), Deep Doorframe Row (tier 4), Single-Arm Doorframe Row (tier 5)
- **Assets needed**: doorframe b-roll from two angles, voiceover.
- **Reuse**: App Store screenshot "A complete back workout. Zero equipment."

## 6 — Wed 2026-09-09 — Zero noise, full session

- **Topic**: Five strength moves that make zero noise, for training while a
  child sleeps on the other side of the wall.
- **Pillar**: Fast tips
- **Why now**: the quiet constraint is FITHER's most structural insight and
  the most common objection to home training in flats.
- **Angle**: the workout industry assumes you can jump. You can't, and you
  don't need to. Tension: silence as a hard requirement, not a preference.
- **Target search phrase**: quiet workout apartment
- **Format**: text on screen over one static shot (a dim living room, a mat)
- **Movements/patterns used**: Glute Bridge, Wall Sit, Wall Push-Up, Lying
  Heel Tap, Knee Plank
- **Assets needed**: one or two dim living room shots, voiceover recorded
  at low, calm level to match the theme.
- **Reuse**: onboarding "Quiet mode" copy; adaptation explainer line.

## What week 01 tests (EXP-001, EXP-002, EXP-003 in content/experiments.md)

Three constraint environments run head to head this week: hotel (1),
kitchen (3) and office (7). Whichever earns the best watch percentage takes
week 02's free slot and becomes the lead App Store screenshot candidate.
The two ladders (2 and 5) test whether progression content out-saves
situation content; if either clears 15 saves per 1000, week 02 gets a
second ladder and the first animation order prioritises those movements.
The reframe (4) is the positioning bet: strong shares or comment agreement
would promote its language toward the paywall headline. If watch percentage
on the two text-on-screen videos (4 and 6) lags environment POV by more
than 10 points, week 02 shifts the mix toward environment POV while the
animation library is still empty.
