First week planned against real data. Three findings set the constraints before
a single topic was chosen.

**Format is decided, not open.** `assets/animation-library.json` still has zero
approved exports (7 candidates sit in coach review), and `data/assets.md` marks
every priority movement Blocked (0 approved clips; Full Plank has 1 and is

and nothing carrying real footage or a photo has proven anything, and the
cocktail path just failed on a sourced photo that was the wrong gender for the
brand persona with no reliable automatic catch. **All 7 videos this week are
motion typography. No environment POV, no cocktail, no probe of either.** That

limiting factor, so there is nothing to learn from spending footage budget this

**Every hook this week is built around a named constraint.** The account draws
55% male viewers on a channel written for women 28 to 45, and the only tracked
search term driving traffic was "incline push ups", which belongs to any
fitness account on the platform. The bar is the 09-14 rewrite: "The floor
creaks. She trains anyway." beats "These five moves make zero noise." because
one of them is a room with a woman in it and the other is a claim. Every single
line below is written to that bar, and every target search phrase names her
situation rather than a movement.

`data/insights.md` reports 0.0% watch across all four rows because `watch_pct`,

and views are noise for the first 60 days by this channel's own measurement
order. Friday's intake needs per-post watch % and per-post saves or the whole
learning loop runs on view counts, which decide nothing.

**Pillar mix**: 3 Constraint, 2 Skill ladder, 1 Reframe, 1 Fast tips. The free
slot goes to Constraint. There is no watch % to award it on, which the rule
resolves as Constraint by default; the only directional signal available (views)
points the same way, since the top three posts by views are all Constraint.
The three Constraint slots deliberately share no environment with week 01:
hotel, kitchen, office and doorframe are all spent, so this week runs the
end of the day beside the bed, a floor with a toddler on it, and a week with
no spare decisions left in it.

**Behind the build** stays out of rotation: the channel is not past month 2.
**Body-only demo** stays out: that unlocks at the week 6 checkpoint, and only

## What week 02 tests

Three experiments run this week: **EXP-006**, **EXP-007** and **EXP-008**, all
registered in `content/experiments.md`.

EXP-006 re-runs the question EXP-004 was voided on, now that all seven topics
ship a carousel and a single on the same date: seven matched pairs, format the
only variable, saves per 1000 views the metric. If the carousel clears the
single by 5 saves per 1000 or more, the next plan written after the data
arrives drops singles to two a week and spends the freed slots on second
carousels. EXP-007 is the one that matters most, because EXP-005 already
decided that the writing is the limiting factor: four of the seven carousels
open on a named constraint (11pm beside the bed, a toddler at rep four, the stairs at 8:40,
quarter end) and three open on a number-led line with no scene in it, which
tests whether cinematic specificity is what earns the save or just what reads
well to us. If the named-constraint group wins by 3 saves per 1000, or the
account's female viewer share climbs 5 points from 42% to 47% or better, then
named-constraint hooks become mandatory on every slot and number-led openings
retire for good. EXP-008 watches the search terms: week 01's only tracked term
was the generic "incline push ups", and every target phrase this week is shaped
like her situation instead, so two or more constraint-shaped tracked terms
would say captions are the lever and zero or one would send the next test to
hashtags instead.

The result that would most change week 03 is EXP-007 going the wrong way. If
the number-led hooks match or beat the named-constraint ones on saves while the
audience stays 55% male, then specificity is not the fix, the audience is
arriving from somewhere the writing does not control, and week 03 stops
rewriting hooks and starts testing distribution surfaces (hashtags, posting
time, search phrasing) instead. The second thing that would change the plan is
another data pull with no per-post watch % or saves in it: two of this week's
three experiments need per-post engagement, and if TikTok Studio still will not
show it, the honest response is to stop registering experiments that the
available data cannot decide.
