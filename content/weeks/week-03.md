# Week 03 — 2026-09-24 to 2026-09-30

Planned 2026-09-26, mid-week. Thursday 24 and Friday 25 shipped only their
"Do these" series deck (written 2026-09-18). Saturday 26 to Wednesday 30 run
the full cadence: three carousels a day, one series deck and two one-offs.

**What the plan stands on.** One data point with real signal: the quarter-end
"Do these 3" carousel, 836 views and 14.4 saves per 1000 in its first hours,
88% women, 96% US, 75% aged 25 to 44, every viewer a non-follower. So every
deck is a gold poster cover on one of the owner's hook formats, plain-English
body, "Name. Reps. One instruction." slides, US words, home and office scenes.
No travel scenes (owner rule). No new numbers have come in since; Friday's
intake is still owed, which is why EXP-007 and EXP-008 stay open.

**Pillar mix for the 15 decks.** 5 Constraint (the series), 4 Skill ladder,
3 Reframe, 3 Fast tips. No day carries two decks from one pillar, and no day
names the same movement in two decks.

**Figures.** Only the seven approved exports carry the figure: Wall Push-Up,
Air Squat, Reverse Lunge, Glute Bridge, Seated Knee Lift, Full Plank,
Doorframe Row. The six newer poses are authored but not rendered or reviewed.

**Five-move series decks** fold the skip note into the structure slide's
footer, because a carousel caps at eight slides.

## Sat 26

1. **Do these 5 every day this week, even with everyone home.** Constraint,
   series. Air Squat, Doorframe Row, Glute Bridge, Wall Push-Up, Full Plank.
   All five patterns and all five figures; the skip note says nothing is
   skipped because the weekend has a floor and a doorframe.
2. **A strong back with no dumbbells: four floor raises, easiest to
   hardest.** Skill ladder (format #24). Shoulder Blade Squeeze, Prone W
   Raise, Prone Y Raise, Prone Y-T-W Raise. Capability at the top: lifting the
   box onto the top shelf and holding it there. Search: back exercises at home
   no dumbbells.
3. **Ten minutes is a complete session. Nobody told you that.** Reframe
   (format #5). The ten minutes is not a warm-up for a real workout that
   never comes. Search: is a 10 minute workout enough.

## Sun 27

   series. Standing Hip Hinge, Wall Push-Up, Wall Sit, Doorframe Lean Row.
   Skips core.
2. **Week one: a plank on the wall. Week eight: a plank on the floor. The

   Enjoy.** Fast tips (format #76). Seated Knee Lift, Shoulder Blade Squeeze,
